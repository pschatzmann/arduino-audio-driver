Detailed Description

Header only C++ driver for the Texas Instruments TLV320AIC3104 low-power stereo audio codec.

Structured to mirror the arduino-audio-driver TLV320AIC3110.h driver. NOTE: the AIC3104 is a member of the older "AIC3x" codec family and has a materially different register map than the AIC3110/TLV320AIC31xx family.

Reference: https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv320aic3104.pdf
